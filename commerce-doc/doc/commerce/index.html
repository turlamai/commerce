Commerce🔗ℹ

TuringLambdaAI

 (require commerce) package: commerce

Public module: commerce

Commerce is a provider-neutral commercial management core: authenticated payment facts enter, deterministic order projections and rights intents leave. It orchestrates payment providers and a Permit issuer without moving payment or authorization policy into Rivet, Glaze, Keel, or product clients.

The default module is pure. Provider adapters explicitly require "commerce/provider.rkt" to construct verified canonical events after their native authentication and semantic checks.
