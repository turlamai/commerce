3 Provider boundary🔗ℹ

Provider adapters require "commerce/provider.rkt" and construct a canonical event only after authenticating the exact raw input, mapping the Offer and subject, checking amount and currency, and performing any active order lookup required by the provider contract.

(make-verified-payment-event

 provider event-id payload-digest kind order-id offer-id

 subject-id amount occurred-at)

 -> payment-event?

Product clients must not call this function.
