11 Readiness and health🔗ℹ

Processes explicitly require "commerce/health.rkt".

(storage-readiness storage #:now now) -> readiness?

One read-only aggregate: schema version, journal and projection counts, pending and processing issuance work with a stale-lease count, the oldest pending availability time, and claim state counts including the window-passed count.

(health-report storage #:now now) -> health-status?

(health-report! storage #:now now) -> health-status?

Classifies the snapshot into typed warnings — ’projection-blocked, ’issuance-lease-expired, ’claim-window-passed — and the logging variant emits them on the commerce topic. Nothing mutates; an HTTP edge would render this value.
